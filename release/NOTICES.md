# Release notices

The acknowledgements the licences of code on the media require. The release
notes carry them as written here, and so does any advertising material that
mentions the feature named.

- Floating-point emulation in the 68040/68060 kernel (package `kern46m`):
  "This product includes software developed by Gordon Ross" and "This
  product includes software developed by the University of California,
  Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory." Full texts in the package's copyright file
  (NETBSD-M68K-FPE.txt among them).
- Heirloom tar (`/usr/sbin/tar`, package `heirtar`): "This product includes
  software developed or owned by Caldera International, Inc." Changes and
  support files by Gunnar Ritter under a zlib-style licence. Full texts in
  `/opt/amix/doc/heirloom-tar/`.
- The prngd entropy daemon (package `prngd`, its `bsd-daemon.c`):
  "This product includes software developed by the University of
  California, Berkeley and its contributors." Full texts in
  `/opt/amix/doc/prngd/LICENSE`.
- Time sync (package `rdate`, its `rdate.c` and `rfc868time.c`): "This
  product includes software developed by Christos Zoulas." Full texts in
  `/opt/amix/doc/rdate/`.

A package whose licence adds such an acknowledgement adds its line here in
the same change.
