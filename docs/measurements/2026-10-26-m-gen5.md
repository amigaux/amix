# M-GEN5 — upgrade, mark-good, rollback, one-shot, try-once (bench, 2026-10-26)

- **Measurement:** M-GEN5 (90 §2): upgrade, commit, mark-good, rollback,
  one-shot previous, and the try-once fallback on a trial that cannot
  reach multi-user. Row S2-41 (`docs/plan/stage-2.md`).
- **Class:** bench, on the project's emulator rig: one lifecycle cell of
  seven boots on one slot. Metal: one row per box per line (S2-47).
- **Inputs:**
  - the kinfo row's vehicle: the Z3660 with autoboot, KD060-Z, generation
    1 good, a 4 MiB store;
  - the box kbootset and the stand-in kernel package made from the same
    KD060-Z kernel (`tests/bench/s2-standin.sh`);
  - for the bad trial, KD060-A, whose root controller is the A4091, which
    the vehicle does not have, committed with `commit --spec`;
  - the recipe `tests/bench/README.md` "S2-41", with `mark-bad 2` on
    boot 5 (the 4 MiB store holds two raw k46 generations).
- **Evidence:** the workspace's `tmp/2026-10-26-s2/s2-41b/N36/`

## 1. Results

The rig runs `kbootset mark-good` after each login, standing in for the end
of run level 2. Its "nothing to mark" lines are left out below.

| Boot | Commands | Seen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `kbootset build; kbootset commit` | "made /dev/kinfo", "generation 2 built", steps 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, "generation 2 committed; power-cycle to boot it" |
| 2 | (mark-good) | `amix-boot: generation 2, kernel unix 1998273 bytes`; "generation 2 is good and the default" |
| 3 | `kbootset rollback` | boots generation 2; "the default is generation 1 (was 2)" |
| 4 | `kbootset next previous` | boots generation 1; "the next boot only is changed" |
| 5 | `kbootset mark-bad 2; kbootset commit --spec bad.spec` | boots generation 2, once, the one-shot; "marked bad and collected"; "generation 3 committed; power-cycle to boot it" |
| 6 | — | `amix-boot: generation 3, kernel unix 1926714 bytes`, the trial. The kernel cannot find its root: `PANIC: vfs_mountroot: cannot mount root: errno 89`, and **it reboots by itself**. The loader runs again in the same rig boot: `amix-boot: L8 trial generation did not reach multi-user (generation 3)`, then generation 1 boots to login |
| 7 | `kbootset status` | generation 1 boots; control sequence 11, default 1, no trial; generation 1 good, generation 3 bad with 1 attempt; generation 2 gone |

After the cell, lsstore of the slot image: `ok`, generation 1 good and
the default, generation 3 bad with 1 attempt, both kernels' CRC-32 ok.

Boot 7's status warned that a generation the size of the default does not
fit (198 656 bytes free). That is the 4 MiB vehicle store: generation 3
stays until a collect. The 64 MiB store of the 1.1 layout does not meet it
(40 §2.1).

## 2. One difference from the recipe: the panic reboots by itself

The recipe expected boot 6 to stop at the panic, and the L8 line on
boot 7. On the machine, the kernel reboots after its panic, so the try-once
fallback happens inside boot 6:
1. the trial's kernel panics;
2. the machine resets;
3. the loader finds the trial attempted and not good, marks it bad (L8),
   and boots generation 1.

The end state is the one expected. The recipe now says so.

The rig's own verdict for the cell is FAIL, with `b6:no-login-prompt`. Its
boot-6 watch waited 160 s for a login, because the panic and the second
loader run come first. The login and boot 6's batch did follow. The row
is judged on its expected lines, not on the rig's boot wait.

## 3. Verdict

**M-GEN5 passes on the bench for the k46 line.** Every step of the
lifecycle behaved as 40 §3 and 10 §7 specify:
- upgrade and commit;
- mark-good;
- rollback;
- the one-shot `next previous`;
- mark-bad;
- a gate-less trial committed with `--spec`;
- the try-once fallback to the good default.

The k30 line follows when `kinfo` in the 030 families (S2-11) and its
kernel package exist. Metal: S2-47.
