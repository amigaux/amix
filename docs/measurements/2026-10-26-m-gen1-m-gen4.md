# M-GEN1, M-GEN4 and M-SIG2 — generation size, kbootset's size, SHA-256 throughput (2026-10-26)

- **Measurements:**
  - M-GEN1: generation size per line;
  - M-GEN4: kbootset's static size per line;
  - M-SIG2: SHA-256 throughput, the bench half.
- **Class:** bench (host computation over the real build outputs; M-SIG2 from the emulator runs of M-LD7)
- **Tool:** `tools/measure/m-gen1.py <workspace>`; `make box` and `m68k-elf-size` for M-GEN4; `tools/measure/axtime.c` for M-SIG2
- **Stage:** 2. Stage 3 re-measures M-GEN1, once root-class modules join the kernel.

## 1. M-GEN1 — a stage-2 generation per kernel family

A stage-2 generation is a static kernel and its manifest. The manifest
takes one 2 048-byte extent; the kernel takes its size rounded up to
2 048. A disk store keeps kernels raw (10 §5.5). "Deflated" is the size on
media that must fit (zlib level 9, the smaller of memory levels 8 and 9,
as mkstore stores it). The kernels are `amix-kerntools/work/k0-chains/<family>`,
read only. The 040/060 families are built as ET_REL; their `brel` form
(`amix-kerntools/tools/elf2brel.py`) is the form the 1.0 floppies carried.

| Family | Form | Raw | Deflated | Generation (raw) | In a 64 MiB store | In a 10 MiB slice |

| K030-U | brel | 1 267 045 | 534 143 | 1 269 760 | 52 | 8 |
| K030-IDE | brel | 1 260 137 | 531 239 | 1 263 616 | 53 | 8 |
| K030-A4091-IDE | brel | 1 262 861 | 532 433 | 1 265 664 | 52 | 8 |

**What it means:**
- The kept set is four generations (pristine, two good, one trial, 40 §1).
- A commit needs room for one more, because the extents of a generation
  it collects become free only after the control-block flip (40 §3.2).
  So a store must hold five.
- The 1.1 layout's 64 MiB store holds at least 32, raw, so it is never
  the limit.
- The 1.0 layout's 10 MiB boot slice holds 5 ET_REL k46 generations, raw:
  exactly enough, with nothing to spare. It holds 6–8 brel or k30
  generations.
- A box that keeps a 1.0-sized boot slice has to stay with brel kernels,
  or with smaller ones. 1.0 boxes reinstall to the 1.1 layout anyway
  (D-3 §5).
- brel is 27 % smaller than ET_REL raw and 17 % smaller deflated. For the
  stage-2 static kernel packages, brel is the smaller choice. The module
  kernels of stage 3 need ET_REL for the module core (20 §3).

## 2. M-GEN4 — kbootset, statically linked

One binary serves both lines: GCC 14 output runs on the 68020 and up
(D-3 §9). Built by `make box` at d87d47d.

| | Bytes |
|---|---|
| text | 110 108 |
| data | 1 764 |
| bss | 249 512 (static buffers: manifests, the inflate state, the parse tables) |
| file, stripped | 119 388 |

The floppy pair's margins (M-FP1: 270 336 and 212 992 bytes free on
volume 2) take it with room to spare. So does the netinstall miniroot,
where it replaces nothing of M-NET1a's set.

## 3. M-SIG2 — SHA-256 throughput (bench half)

From the M-LD7 runs (`docs/measurements/2026-10-26-m-ld7-m-ld8.md`):
- On the cycle-exact 25 MHz 68030 model, the SHA-256 of `loader/common`
  (the code both apkg and kbootset carry) runs at **540 KiB/s** from fast
  memory.
- A `kbootset check` of a k46 generation (about 2 MB) costs about 3.8 s
  of hashing on that machine, a k30 one about 2.3 s.
- apkg's check of a 700 KB kernel datastream costs about 1.3 s.
- The 68060 model ran at emulator speed, so the 68060 figure is metal
  (S2-47).
