# M-FP1 (static) and M-NET1d — the floppy pair with real kernels (2026-10-25)

- **Measurements:** M-FP1, static part (floppy-pair fit per line in the RAM-disk profile); M-NET1d (store overhead and usable bytes per DD volume)
- **Class:** bench (host computation): real floppy pairs written by `mkstore -f`, read back by `lsstore` and by boot2's own reader on the host (`tests/host/b2medium.c`)
- **Tool:** `tools/measure/m-fp1.sh <workspace> <netdir> <outdir>` (this repository); it runs `tools/measure/m-net1.py` for the miniroot archives
- **Rule:** the netinstall floppy pair (form A) ships if it fits two volumes with a margin of at least 5 % (`docs/spec/50-media.md` §5)

Paths below are relative to the Amix project workspace.

The archive stands in for the RAM-disk image (`10-boot-loader.md` §5.4): a
filesystem image of the same files adds metadata and block padding, and the
padding is zeros that deflate to almost nothing (as in M-NET1).

## 2. M-NET1d — overhead and usable bytes per volume

| Volume | Fixed content | Data starts at | Usable bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | boot1, two store headers, boot2, one control block, each rounded to 2 048 | 0x7800 today (boot2 26 556 B); 0x8000 with boot2 at its 28 KiB limit | 870 400 (868 352) |
| 2 | two store headers | 0x800 | 899 072 |
| Pair | | | **1 769 472** (1 767 424) |

That is 12 288 B (14 336 B) less than the budget of record of 2 × 890 880 B
that M-NET1 used. Each generation also costs its manifest reservation,
(spec lines + 2) × 256 bytes rounded up to 2 048, which is 4 096 B here. Each
member also loses up to 2 047 B of padding to the next 2 048-byte boundary.

**Finding: a deflated miniroot is larger than one volume.** The trimmed
miniroots deflate to 964 903 and 928 257 bytes, against the 899 072 usable
bytes of volume 2. The first `mkstore -f` refused every configuration
("member larger than a volume"). The format is now: an extent that runs
past the end of its volume continues at 0x800 of the next volume, and boot2
asks for that volume in the middle of the member. The writer reserves each
manifest ahead of its members, so a pair boots with one disk change, not
three. This is a spec change after the freeze (`10-boot-loader.md` §4.3).

## 3. M-FP1 (static) — the fit

RAM-disk profile, one generation, kernel and miniroot both raw-deflate at
level 9 as `mkstore` stores them:

| Line | Engine | Kernel stored | Miniroot stored | Free on volume 2 | Margin of the pair |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| k30 | trimmed | 534 579 | 964 903 | 262 144 | **14.8 %** |
| k46 | trimmed | 626 320 | 928 257 | 208 896 | **11.8 %** |
| k30 | full 1.0 engine | — | — | short by about 4 KiB | does not fit |
| k46 | full 1.0 engine | — | — | short by about 56 KiB | does not fit |

Both trimmed pairs verify with `lsstore`. On each, boot2's reader
(`loader/boot2/main.c`, run on the host against a simulated drive) reads the
manifest and kernel from volume 1. It then asks for volume 2 inside the
miniroot and refuses a blank disk, a disk of another set and volume 1 again.
It accepts volume 2, and inflates and CRC-checks every member: one volume
change per boot.

Against M-NET1b (k30 +16.1 %, k46 +13.0 %), the margins drop by the store
overhead above, the manifest and padding, and 5 KB of deflate difference.
`mkstore` uses zlib memory level 9, which on this input is 4 969 B larger
than level 8.

## 4. Verdict

M-NET1d is closed: 870 400 + 899 072 usable bytes per pair with today's
boot2, and 868 352 + 899 072 at the boot2 limit. M-FP1 (static) is closed
for the RAM-disk profile with the trimmed engine: both lines fit with
margins above 5 % (k30 14.8 %, k46 11.8 %). The full 1.0 engine does not fit
either line, which confirms the trim chosen in M-NET1. Form A stands.
