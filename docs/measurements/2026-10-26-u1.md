# U1 — the context at dmainit and at the root-mount hook (2026-10-26)

- **Measurement:** U1 (90 §3, 30 §3): the status register, whether the
  clock ticks, and the largest `KM_NOSLEEP` allocation at `dmainit` and at
  the kernel's hook in `rth1_mountroot`. Stage 4's root-class `_load` will
  run there (`marc_root` sits earlier in the same function, in the same
  process-0 context).
- **Class:** bench (amiberry), plan item S3-41.
- **Tool:** U1 kernels, each line's kernel of record rebuilt with
  `-DDLM_U1` (core patch 0023, test only; never packaged):
  - k46: the port's U1 kernel `5c4fad37`, build `68060-261026-60`, bound at
    0x08000000 in a 128 MB bank;
  - k30: `ce689ab4`, build `68030-261026-02`, bound at 0x04000000 in a
    64 MB bank.

  Each booted once as a generation by spec, then marked bad and collected.
- **Evidence:** the Bench's runs N77 (k46) and N78 (k30), console frames.

## 1. Results

| | k46 dmainit | k46 hook | k30 dmainit | k30 hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| status register | 0x2704 | 0x2700 | 0x2704 | 0x2704 |
| the clock (4 000 000 spins) | stood still | stood still | stood still | stood still |
| `kmem_zalloc` `KM_NOSLEEP` 4 096 … 1 048 576 | gave memory | gave memory | gave memory | gave memory |
| `kmem_zalloc` `KM_NOSLEEP` 2 097 152 | gave memory | gave memory | **failed** | **failed** |

- The status register is supervisor mode at priority 7 at every point.
  The low bits (0x4, Z) are only the condition codes the probe happened
  to leave.
- `lbolt` does not move at either point on either line: no clock
  interrupt, so no wakeup, no `timeout`, no sleep can end there.
- The largest single `KM_NOSLEEP` allocation:
  - k46: at least 2 MiB at 128 MB. That is the probe's ceiling, and the
    kernel's may be higher.
  - k30: 1 MiB at 64 MB; 2 MiB fails.
- Both boots went on to multi-user.

## 2. What it decides

**The root-class context (30 §3, for stage 4):**
- `_load` never sleeps and arms no timeout. `KM_SLEEP` is **not safe**
  there: priority 7 and a stopped clock mean nothing could wake it. Every
  allocation is `KM_NOSLEEP` and checks for failure.
- A single allocation of **at most 1 MiB** is safe on both lines (k30 at
  64 MB, its maximum). k30 refuses 2 MiB. A root-class module that needs
  more allocates it in pieces of 1 MiB or less, or later, from its first
  open in process context.
- The module images themselves need no kmem at this point: boot2 placed
  them in the arena (C3-4). The arena's capacity is sized by M-MOD1,
  independent of U1.

**OQ-30.1** (is a no-sleep mode needed at `dmainit`, or only at
`marc_root`): both points have the same context. Nothing that runs there
may sleep. Patch 3 (the no-sleep mode) belongs to stage 4 with
`marc_root`. No stage-3 load runs at either point: boot-class loads run
from the first sysinit entry (U15, core patch 0017).

**No second probe is needed.**
- k30's figure (1 MiB yes, 2 MiB no) is enough for the rule above, so a
  finer step between them would change nothing.
- k46 above 2 MiB matters for no stage-4 piece: a module image is at most
  2 MiB (C3-1) and lives in the arena.

If a root-class driver ever needs one allocation over 1 MiB, measure that
size on k30 before relying on it.
