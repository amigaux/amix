## 3. Verdict

**The bench half of M-LD5 is closed.** The menu reads the keyboard
through keyboard.device, the left button from CIA-A, and the serial
console byte by byte. It honours the first-release rule for the mouse,
the rollback key in the menu window, the toggles, and the `r` key's
refusal.

Still open, metal (S1-26): the same rows through the reference machines'
own keyboard and mouse converters, and phantom reads on a marginal card.
