# M-LD1 and M-LD3 — what the strap checks in a bootblock; boot1 with no valid boot2 (bench half, 2026-10-26)

- **Measurements:**
  - M-LD1: which bootblock header fields the Kickstart strap checks, on a
    floppy and on an RDB `UNI\0` partition, per Kickstart version;
  - M-LD3: what happens when the loader cannot continue (boot1 with no
    valid boot2).
- **Class:** bench, on the project's emulator rig: one cold boot per cell
  with serial capture. The Z3660 boot ROM half and both boxes are metal
  (S1-26).
- **Inputs:** `tests/bench/m-ld1.sh` (seven bootblocks, each differing
  from the good one in one field; the floppies; the `dd` lines for the
  disks; `fd-<line>-b2bad.adf`)
- **Evidence:** the workspace's `tmp/2026-10-26-s1-measure/m-ld1/` (`VERDICTS.txt`, `cells/`), 96 cells

## 1. Cells

Twelve columns:
- DF0 with the k30 floppy and with the k46 floppy;
- the A3000 WD, Gayle IDE, A4091 68030 and A4091 68060 rows (the
  N19–N26 vehicles);
- each on Kickstart 3.1 and on 3.2.x.

Each column booted the seven bootblock variants (84 cells), plus M-LD3's
damaged-boot2 vehicle (12 cells).

## 2. M-LD1 results — the same on all twelve columns

| Variant | Changed field | Verdict | What Kickstart did on refusal |
|---|---|---|---|
| good | — | ACCEPTED | — |
| dos1 | magic `DOS\1` | ACCEPTED | — |
| uni0 | magic `UNI\0` | REFUSED | disks: the insert-disk screen; DF0: the next boot node |
| nodos | magic `BAD\0` | REFUSED | as uni0 |
| sum | checksum off by one | REFUSED | as uni0 |
| third0 | third longword 0 | ACCEPTED | — |
| notrailer | last longword 0 | ACCEPTED | — |

ACCEPTED means the serial log showed the loader's `amix-boot 1:` banner.

On Kickstart 3.1 and 3.2.x, on a floppy and on an RDB `UNI\0` partition,
the strap checks two things:
- the first three bytes are `DOS`, with the fourth byte, the type, not
  checked;
- the bootblock checksum.

It does not check the third longword or the trailer. A bootblock whose
magic is `UNI\0`, the partition's own DosType, counts as no bootblock at
all.

When it refuses a disk partition, Kickstart shows its insert-disk screen.
When it refuses DF0, it moves on to the next boot node (a disk behind DF0
then boots through its own loader).

## 3. M-LD3 results

On all twelve columns, the floppy or store whose only boot2 copy is
damaged printed exactly

```
amix-boot1: B3 no valid boot2
amix-boot1: stopped. Reset; both mouse buttons at reset: Kickstart menu
```

and stopped. boot1 does not return to the strap, so no AN_BootError
reset loop follows (S1-16 found that loop with the Z3660 chain, and
10 §3.1 step 6 was changed after it).

## 4. Verdict

**The bench halves of M-LD1 and M-LD3 are closed.**
- Our bootblock framing passes: `DOS\0`, the checksum, 0x370, and the
  trailer kept for the A3000 SuperKickstart. The strap would accept a
  bootblock without the last two, but they cost nothing and the A3000
  SuperKickstart path reads its trailer (10 §2.1).
- boot1's failure path stops cleanly on every controller and both
  Kickstarts.

Still open, metal (S1-26): the Z3660 boot ROM's checks, and both boxes.
